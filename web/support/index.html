Support

We're happy to help. Most questions are answered below — for anything else, email us and we'll reply within 2–3 business days.

📧 support@ftmpapp.app

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. Open Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device, or manage subscriptions here, to change or cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the current period.

Restore a purchase

New device, or reinstalled the App? Open Settings → FTMP Pro → Restore Purchases in the App (or the Restore button on the upgrade screen) to restore an active subscription or the lifetime purchase.

Delete your team & data

  • In the App: open My Team, tap the role badge (top-right) or the profile screen, and choose Leave / Delete Team to remove the team from your device and stop cloud sharing.
  • Reset everything: Settings → Reset All Data clears local progress and data.
  • Cloud deletion request: to have cloud team data associated with your device deleted from our servers, email support@ftmpapp.app.

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The games, database and stats all work offline. A connection is only needed to share or join a live team with a code.

How do I unlock Pro for free?

Keep playing to earn XP — reaching Level 15 unlocks all Pro features at no cost.

Where does the football data come from?

The app ships with a bundled database used for entertainment. Names are used for identification only; FTMP is not affiliated with any club, league or competition.

Why is a feature locked?

Some features are part of FTMP Pro. You can subscribe, or unlock them by reaching Level 15.
